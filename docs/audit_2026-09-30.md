## (4) Score

The reviewers scored 5.5, 6.5, 6.5, 5, 5 and 5; my overall score is 5.5/10. The empirical and preregistration discipline is better than usual in VSA work. What drags the score down is framing and edge cases:
- a wrong test count;
- novelty that overlaps FKS 2018 and Kleyko 2023;
- "exact" that fails on cycles, with an 11–12 point gap on a biclique;
- two shipped bugs that give answers that are badly wrong without raising an error.

Two steps would raise it:
- **To about 7:** complete Tier A (roughly 2–3 days of work).
- **To about 7.5–8:** also:
  - count 4-cycles in the real samples and show whether they explain the hub residual;
  - run the Test 10 ablation;
  - compare against a Gaussian FKS integral at the paper's D, N and M, so the binomial refinement has a measured size;
  - a clean replicate.py covering all eleven tests.

Beyond that, the ceiling depends on one thing. The work needs a result that is new rather than a refinement, for example modelling the GF(2) dependence exactly on knowledge graphs.
